# NNNN. Short title in the imperative ("Use X for Y")

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deferred | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, constraints, and what makes this a real decision
rather than an obvious default. State facts, not the choice yet.

## Decision

What we chose, stated plainly. One paragraph.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why it was rejected.
- **Option B** — why it was rejected.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder. The accepted trade-off, stated honestly,
and **what would change this decision** (e.g. at scale, or given different requirements).
